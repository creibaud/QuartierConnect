The **Bookings** page gathers every paid service request that concerns you: the ones you sent to a neighbor, and the ones a neighbor sent you. It is your tracking point between the moment a booking is requested and the moment the exchange closes with a signed contract and a points transfer.

Each booking shows up as a card recalling the essentials: the **service** involved, the **number of points** at stake, the **date** of the request and its current **status**.

## Two tabs: Received and Sent

The page splits into two tabs, and the counter in brackets tells you how many bookings sit in each:

- **Received**: the requests a neighbor sent you on one of your paid services. This is where you **accept** or **decline** a pending request.
- **Sent**: the bookings you requested from a neighbor yourself. You follow their progress there and can **cancel** them.

One and the same exchange therefore appears in the **Sent** tab for the requester, and in the **Received** tab for the person who will provide the service. On opening, the page shows the **Received** tab; if you only have sent requests, it takes you straight to **Sent**.

## Requesting a booking

You do not create a booking from this page: it always starts from a **paid service**. From the details of a paid listing, on the [Services between neighbors](/en/services) page, you request the booking. The **number of points is calculated automatically** from the service's rate (its duration and its multiplier, if any): you do not have to set it yourself.

The request then appears right away in your **Sent** tab, with the status **Pending**.

A few safeguards apply when the request is made:

- only **paid** services can be booked;
- you cannot book **your own** listing;
- a **closed** service can no longer be booked;
- you cannot send a second request while a booking is still **active** (pending or accepted) on the same service.

## A booking's status

Each card carries a **status** telling you where the booking stands:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| Pending | The request is sent; the service owner has not made a decision yet. |
| Accepted | The owner accepted: a contract has just been generated. |
| Declined | The owner turned the request down. No points are exchanged. |
| Cancelled | The booking was cancelled before it closed. No points are exchanged. |
| Completed | The contract was fully signed and the points transfer settled. |

These statuses always follow the same order: a booking starts **Pending**, then takes one of the outcomes below.

## Accepting or declining a received request

In the **Received** tab, any request still **Pending** offers you two buttons: **Accept** and **Decline**.

- **Accept** moves the booking to the **Accepted** status and triggers the automatic creation of a **contract** between you and the requester. The matching points are **reserved** from then on, pending the signatures. A **View contract** button then appears on the card to go straight to the document.
- **Decline** closes the request with the **Declined** status. Nothing is charged, and the requester is notified.

> Only the service owner can accept or decline a received request. If an action cannot go through, a short **"Action failed"** message tells you so, and nothing changes.

## Cancelling a booking

From the **Sent** tab, a **Cancel** button is available on your bookings as long as they are **Pending** or **Accepted**. Cancelling moves the booking to the **Cancelled** status.

If the booking had already produced a contract, cancelling **releases the reserved points** and **cancels** the associated contract. One important limit applies though:

> A booking can no longer be cancelled once the **contract is fully signed**. The payment being settled by then, the exchange is considered closed and the booking moves to the **Completed** status. Cancelling stays possible **as long as the contract is not fully signed**.

## From booking to contract to points transfer

An accepted booking is not the end of the road: it opens the way to the contract that formalizes the exchange, then to the points transfer that closes it.

1. You **accept** a received request: a **contract** is generated automatically, and the points are **reserved**.
2. Both parties **sign** the contract. Signing happens in three steps (reading the terms, drawing your handwritten signature, then confirming with your six-digit code).
3. As soon as the **last signature** lands, the **points transfer** fires automatically and seals the exchange. The booking then flips to the **Completed** status.

For everything about signing, tracking and the contract PDF, see the [Contracts and signing](/en/contrats) page. To understand how the transfer credits and debits accounts, see the [Points and rewards](/en/points) page.

## In short

- The **Bookings** page tracks your paid service requests in two tabs: **Received** (to accept or decline) and **Sent** (to follow or cancel).
- A booking always starts from a **paid service**; its points are calculated **automatically** from the rate.
- The **status** (Pending, Accepted, Declined, Cancelled, Completed) tells you where each exchange stands.
- Accepting a request **generates a contract** and **reserves the points**; the transfer settles **on the last signature**.
- Cancelling stays possible **as long as the contract is not fully signed**.
