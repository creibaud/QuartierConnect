You will find here short answers to the questions residents ask most often. Where useful, each answer points to the exact place in the app where you can act.

## Login and two-factor authentication

Your account is protected by two-factor authentication: at every login, after your email and your password, you enter a six-digit code generated by an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Aegis, FreeOTP, etc.).

### I lost my code, my authenticator app no longer generates the right code

The six-digit code is never fixed: it changes every 30 seconds. If the code is rejected, first check that your phone's time is set automatically, then wait for a new code to appear before trying again.

As long as you are logged in, you can manage your two-factor authentication from the **Profile** page, in the **Security** card, which brings together password and two-factor authentication management.

### I changed phones

On your new phone, install an authenticator app again, then set up two-factor authentication once more from the **Profile** page, **Security** card. You scan the QR code offered (or copy the secret displayed), then confirm with the first six-digit code, exactly as you did when you signed up.

> Good habit: set up your two-factor authentication again **before** parting with your old phone, while you are still logged in.

## My address and my neighborhood

To access local content, your account must be attached to a neighborhood, determined from your address.

### My address is "outside coverage"

If your address is recognised but falls in no neighborhood covered yet, you land on a **"pending coverage"** screen. Your address is then recorded so the administration team can extend coverage to your area. You can correct your address at any time if you made a mistake.

## How do points work?

The **Points** page shows your balance and the history of your transactions: points received, points sent and service payments.

- You can **transfer points** to a neighbor: search for the recipient, enter an amount and an optional note, then confirm. Your balance and your history update immediately.
- Some services between neighbors are **paid in points**. The number of points is computed from the service's rate; a multiplier may be attached to it. When a paid booking is accepted, a contract is generated: it is the **last signature** (the one that makes the contract signed by both parties) that automatically triggers the points transfer and seals the exchange. The points therefore move at the final signature, not afterwards.

## The privacy of my data (GDPR)

Your personal data is protected and you keep control of it from the **Profile** page, **Privacy (GDPR)** card.

### Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. The **Export my data** option downloads all of your personal data in a structured file (right to portability, article 20 of the GDPR). The archive contains, among other things, your profile, your incidents, your services, your events, your contracts, your points and your conversations.

Some sensitive information is **never** included in the export, such as your password (which is in any case only stored in encrypted form) or your two-factor authentication secret.

## How do I delete my account?

From the **Profile** page, **Privacy (GDPR)** card, choose **Delete my account**. Deletion is **permanent** and you must confirm it by entering your six-digit code, to avoid any accidental deletion.

On deletion, you are logged out immediately. Your incident reports may be kept for as long as moderation requires, while the rest of your profile is removed from the platform.
