The **Recommendations** page brings together, in one place, everything QuartierConnect suggests you discover: **services** offered near you, upcoming **events** in your neighborhood, and **neighbors** worth getting in touch with. These are personalized suggestions, built from your neighborhood activity — with nothing to ask for.

## Where to find them

Two paths lead to your recommendations:

- **From the menu**, the **"Recommendations"** entry (recognizable by its little sparkles icon) opens the full list.
- **From your dashboard**, the **"Recommendations for you"** card shows a preview of the first suggestions. One click takes you to the detailed list.

Each suggestion appears as a card, with an icon matching its type, its name, a badge (**Service**, **Event** or **Neighbor**) and a short sentence explaining **why** it is being suggested to you.

## What QuartierConnect suggests

Recommendations fall into three families, each with its own reason:

- **A service from your neighborhood** — *"Service offered in your neighborhood"*. A service listing near you that you haven't used yet.
- **An upcoming event** — *"Upcoming event near you"*. A neighborhood gathering still to come that you aren't already attending.
- **An event your neighbors like** — *"Popular with neighbors who share your interests"*. An event enjoyed by neighbors who showed interest in the same gatherings as you.
- **A trusted neighbor** — *"Trusted neighbor, known for helping out"*. A resident of your neighborhood who has already made a name for themselves helping others around them.

The most relevant suggestions rise to the top of the list, and the list stays deliberately short to keep to the essentials.

## How these suggestions are built

Nothing mysterious: QuartierConnect simply relies on what you already do in the app. Three things feed your recommendations.

- **Your neighborhood** determines what is suggested nearby: the services and events attached to your area.
- **Your interests** are inferred from the [events](/en/evenements) you marked yourself interested in or are attending. They help spot the neighbors who resemble you and the events they like.
- **Neighborhood mutual aid** highlights the neighbors recognized for the services they have provided, once the [contracts](/en/contrats) have run their course.

> **Good to know:** these suggestions naturally leave out anything there is no longer any point in suggesting — a service you have already used, an event you are already attending, or your own listings. So you won't see things you already know coming back.

## Acting on a suggestion

Each card takes you straight to where the action happens:

- A **service** or **event** suggestion leads you to the matching page — [Services](/en/services) or [Events](/en/evenements) — to find out more and, if you want, respond to it or sign up.
- A **neighbor** suggestion offers a **"Contact"** button. One click opens a conversation in your [messaging](/en/messagerie), ready to start.

## Why don't I see anything?

If the list shows **"No recommendations yet"**, it simply means QuartierConnect doesn't have enough to go on yet to suggest anything relevant. The fix fits in a single sentence, the one the app whispers to you: **take part in neighborhood life to receive tailored suggestions**.

In practice, the more you mark your interest in events, and the more services you exchange with your neighbors, the sharper and richer your recommendations get. This is a page that grows with you.
