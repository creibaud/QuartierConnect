The dashboard is your **home page**: it is the screen that greets you as soon as you sign
in and your account is attached to a neighborhood. It gathers in a single place the gist
of what is happening around you, in the form of clear cards you can go through at a
glance. This page explains what each of those cards shows.

At the top, a welcome message greets you **by your first name**, along with your email
address and a badge recalling your role. Below it unfold the map of your neighborhood,
then a series of overview cards that link out to the platform's main pages.

## The map of your neighborhood

The first card shows an **interactive plan centered on your neighborhood**. It draws the
boundary of the neighborhood you are assigned to and places markers on it so you can see,
in an instant, what is going on near you:

- the **service offers** and **service requests** that have a position set;
- the located **events**;
- the **neighborhood incidents** reported on the map;
- your **home**, if your address could be positioned.

A **legend** recalls what each type of marker stands for. Only the items whose position is
known show up: a service or an event without a locatable address is still published, but
without a point on the plan. For everything about the neighborhood boundary and the notion
of coverage, see the page
[The neighborhood map](/en/carte).

> If your neighborhood does not have a boundary defined yet, the map is not displayed: the
> rest of the dashboard, however, is still there.

## Your points at a glance

One card highlights your **points balance**, the neighborhood's mutual-help currency. The
figure is shown in large type; as long as no balance is available, a plain dash takes its
place. A **"View history"** link takes you straight to the page
[Points and rewards](/en/points), where you find the detail of all your movements.

## Your recent activity

Right next to it, the recent activity card lists your **latest point transactions**. Each
line indicates the neighbor involved and the amount:

- in **green with a `+`** for the points you received;
- in **grey with a `−`** for the points you sent or spent.

This overview only shows the most recent movements; the **"See all"** button opens the
full history on the Points page.

## Ongoing votes

This card lists the **neighborhood consultations under way**. For each vote, you see its
title, the **number of participants** already counted and, where applicable, its **closing
date**.

- If you have already voted, a **"Voted"** badge confirms it.
- Otherwise, a button invites you to **respond** and takes you to the Votes page.

Only votes still open appear here: as soon as a consultation is closed, it makes way for
the next ones.

## Upcoming events

The events card shows the **next local get-togethers**, sorted from the nearest to the
furthest away. Each event shows its **date** (day and month), its title, its place when
one is set and a **category** badge. Events that have already passed are not listed. The
**"See all"** button opens the Events page.

## Neighborhood services

This card gives an overview of the **services offered around you**: for each one, its
title, a short description and its **category**. It is a quick way to spot a helping hand
available or a request you could answer. The link leads to the page
[Services between neighbors](/en/services).

## Recommendations for you

Finally, one card suggests content **picked for you**: a service, an event or a neighbor.
Each recommendation shows a name, a **reason** (for example a service in your
neighborhood, an event nearby, shared interests or a trusted neighbor) and a badge
indicating its type. The **"See all"** button opens the Recommendations page.

## When a card is empty

The dashboard adapts to your neighborhood's activity. If a section has nothing to show yet
(no ongoing vote, no upcoming event, no transaction, etc.), the card shows a **friendly
welcome message** rather than a blank, with a short hint about what will appear there one
day. And if loading fails momentarily, a **"Retry"** button lets you reload the card
concerned without leaving the page.

## In short

| Card | What it shows | To which page |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| Neighborhood map | Your neighborhood, the services, events, incidents and your home | Map |
| Your points | Your points balance | Points |
| Recent activity | Your latest point transactions | Points |
| Ongoing votes | The consultations under way and your participation | Votes |
| Upcoming events | The next local get-togethers | Events |
| Neighborhood services | An overview of service offers and requests | Services |
| Recommendations | Suggestions picked for you | Recommendations |

The dashboard is not a page you linger on: it is a **starting point**. Each card gives you
the gist and opens the door to the matching section, so you can explore your neighborhood
at your own pace.
