Points are QuartierConnect's mutual-help currency. They let you thank a neighbor who
helped you out and pay for the services offered in the neighborhood. This page explains
how to earn them, where to check your balance and your history, and how to transfer some
to a neighbor.

At a glance, points always follow the same cycle: you **earn** them by helping out, they
feed your **balance**, then you **spend** them to pay for a service or **transfer** them
straight to a neighbor. That neighbor can in turn send them back around the neighborhood.

## How do you earn points?

You earn points when a neighbor calls on you and the exchange is completed. On
QuartierConnect, services between neighbors come in three types:

- **free**: you help out with no points in return;
- **paid (in points)**: the service is settled with points;
- **exchange**: you help each other out.

For a paid service, the flow goes like this:

1. You publish a service listing with its title, description, category and type.
2. An interested neighbor **responds** to your listing, then requests a **booking**.
   The number of points is calculated automatically from the service's points rate.
3. You **accept** the booking: a **contract** is then generated automatically.
4. Both parties **sign** the contract. The **points transfer** is triggered
   **automatically as soon as the last signature is added** and seals the exchange.

That final signature is what credits your balance: the points move as soon as the
contract is complete, with no extra step to confirm. Conversely, when you are the one
requesting a paid service, the same mechanism debits your points to the benefit of
the neighbor who helped you.

## Checking your balance and your history

Your balance is visible in two places.

- On the **dashboard** (home page), a card shows a summary of your points balance along
  with your latest transactions.
- On the **Points** page, you get your full balance and the **detailed history of your
  transactions**.

The history gathers every movement on your account:

- **points received**,
- **points sent**,
- **service payments**.

> Good to know: your balance and your history are updated immediately after each
> operation. So the amount you see is always up to date.

## Transferring points to a neighbor

You can send points straight to a neighbor from the **Points** page, without waiting for
a service. Handy to thank someone or to share your balance.

To make a transfer:

1. Open the **Points** page.
2. **Search for the recipient** among your neighbors.
3. Enter the **amount** to transfer.
4. Optionally add a **note** (it is optional) to explain why you are sending them.
5. **Confirm** the transfer.

As soon as you confirm, the points leave your account for your neighbor's. Your
**balance** and your **history** are updated right away, and the operation appears in
your transactions as points sent. On their side, the recipient sees the movement appear
as points received.

A transfer therefore has only two possible outcomes: either it goes through and the
points change accounts, or it is refused and **no points move**.

### If the transfer does not go through

A transfer can be refused. In that case, **no points leave your account** and a message
explains why:

- **"Insufficient balance for this transfer"**: the amount requested is more than you
  have. Check your balance and try again with a lower amount.
- **"You cannot transfer points to yourself"**: you entered your own account as the
  recipient. Pick another neighbor.
- **"This recipient does not exist"**: the target account is no longer available.
- **"Unable to transfer points"**: a temporary problem interrupted the operation. Try
  again in a moment.

## In short

| Action | Where it happens | Effect on your balance |
|--------|------------------|------------------------|
| Providing a paid service | Bookings and Contracts | Credited on the contract's last signature |
| Paying for a service | Bookings and Contracts | Debited on the contract's last signature |
| Checking balance and history | Dashboard and Points page | None |
| Transferring points | Points page | Debited immediately |

Points reflect the mutual-help life of your neighborhood: the more you help out, the
more you build up so you can, in turn, call on your neighbors.
