Welcome to QuartierConnect, the online space for your neighborhood. This page walks you through creating your account, securing your sign-in and joining your neighborhood. Take all the time you need: every step is simple.

## Overview

Getting started always follows the same path: you create your account, you enable two-factor authentication by scanning a QR code, then you enter your address so you can be attached to your neighborhood. The diagram below sums up that path.

## Step 1: create your account

Registration happens in two stages on the sign-up page. The first stage is filling in your profile.

You provide:

- your **first name** and your **last name**;
- your **email address**;
- your **password**, typed twice for confirmation;
- your **phone number**.

The form automatically checks the format of your email, the minimum password length and that both password entries match.

### Consent, a required step

Before continuing, you must tick the **GDPR consent box**. Without it, account creation is blocked. This is your agreement for your data to be processed.

## Step 2: enable two-factor authentication

To protect your account, QuartierConnect uses **two-factor authentication** (also called TOTP). On top of your password, a six-digit code is asked for at every sign-in. That code changes every few seconds, which makes your account far harder to break into.

Once your profile is validated, the application displays a **QR code** along with the matching secret in plain text, which you can copy.

To enable it:

1. Install an **authenticator app** on your phone if you don't already have one. The best known are Google Authenticator, Aegis and FreeOTP.
2. Open that app and **scan the QR code** shown on screen. If you prefer, you can enter the secret by hand by copying it.
3. Your authenticator app then generates a **first six-digit code**. Enter it in QuartierConnect to confirm the pairing and finish creating your account.

> Keep access to your authenticator app safe: it is what produces the code asked for at every sign-in.

## Step 3: sign in

Once your account is created, signing in takes two simple steps:

1. Enter your **email address** and your **password**.
2. Enter the **six-digit code** shown in your authenticator app.

If your credentials are wrong or the code is invalid, a clear error message appears to help you fix it.

## Step 4: join your neighborhood

To reach local content (services between neighbors, events, votes and so on), your account has to be attached to a neighborhood. After your first sign-in, if no address is associated with your account yet, the application shows an **address entry screen with autocomplete**.

Enter your address using the suggestions offered. Two cases are possible.

- **Your address is in a covered neighborhood.** Your account is automatically attached to that neighborhood, and you reach your dashboard right away.
- **Your address is recognised but outside every covered neighborhood.** You are then taken to a "pending coverage" screen. Your address is saved so the administration team can extend coverage to your area.

In every case, you can **correct your address at any time** if you made a mistake or if you move.
