## What QuartierConnect is for

QuartierConnect is a collaborative platform built for the residents of a single neighborhood. The idea fits in three words: **neighbors, services and good spirits**. It brings neighborhood mutual aid online: helping out around your home, reporting what is going wrong, taking part in local life and staying in touch with your neighbors.

This help is written for you, the resident. From your browser, you reach your personal space and everything happening in your neighborhood.

## What you can do

### Your account

You create your account in a few steps: your profile, your consent to the use of your data, then turning on **two-factor authentication** to protect your access. At each login, you enter your password and then a six-digit code generated by an authenticator app on your phone.

### Your neighborhood

To reach local content, your account is attached to a neighborhood through your address. If your address falls outside the neighborhoods covered, it is recorded so the team can extend the coverage. A **dashboard** then greets you with an interactive map, your points, the open votes, the upcoming events and some recommendations.

### Services between neighbors

You can post a service **offer** or **request** (gardening, handyman, transport, shopping, childcare, IT support, etc.), free, paid in points or as an exchange. An interested neighbor responds, then, for a paid service, books it. You accept the booking, a **contract** is generated, and the exchange is settled with a points transfer.

### Contracts and signing

The Contracts page gathers the contracts you have created or that you need to sign. Signing happens in three stages: you read the terms again, you draw your handwritten signature, then you confirm with your six-digit code. Every contract keeps a reliable record of all its events.

### Events and votes

The Events page shows local life (culture, sport, etc.) as a list or a calendar: you express your **interest** or propose a new event. The Votes page lets you take part in neighborhood consultations and follow the results live.

### Incidents

You report a problem (illegal dumping, broken street lighting, etc.) with a title, a description, a category and a point on the map. Each incident follows a status: open, in progress or resolved.

### Messaging

Messaging connects you with a neighbor in real time. You exchange **text messages**, attach **photos and files**, and can even record **voice messages**.

### Points

Your points stand for a service credit. The Points page shows your balance and the history of your exchanges, and lets you **transfer points** to a neighbor.

## Your personal data

You stay in control of your information. From your profile, you manage your security and your privacy: you can **export all of your data** or **delete your account** permanently.

## How to find your way around this help

Each major feature gets its own page. Start with whatever interests you most, then explore the rest at your own pace.

- **Your account**: signup, login and security.
- **Services between neighbors**: offering, responding, booking.
- **Contracts**: creating and signing a contract.
- **Events and votes**: taking part in neighborhood life.
- **Incidents**: reporting an incident near your home.
- **Messaging**: talking with your neighbors.
- **Points**: tracking your balance and transferring points.
- **Profile and data**: managing your personal information.

> Welcome among your neighbors. Take your time to explore, one service at a time.
