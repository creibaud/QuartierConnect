The **Profile** page brings together everything about you: your identity, your account
security and the control of your personal data. You reach it from your account menu.
This page walks you through each of these settings step by step, without the jargon.

## Overview

Your profile is laid out as **cards**, which you go through from top to bottom:

- **Profile**: your photo, your first name and your last name.
- **Neighborhood**: the address that links you to your neighborhood.
- **Security**: your password and your two-factor authentication.
- **Email address**: the email you sign in with.
- **Phone**: your number, optional.
- **Privacy (GDPR)**: exporting and deleting your data.

One principle runs through this whole page: **every sensitive change** (password, email,
phone, account deletion) asks you for the **six-digit code** from your authenticator app.
That code is what guarantees the change really comes from you, even if someone got hold
of your screen for a moment.

## Editing your profile information

The **Profile** card shows your photo, your full name, your email address and a label
indicating your role (for example "Resident"). To change your name or your photo:

1. Click **Edit** at the top of the card.
2. Fill in your **first name** and your **last name**.
3. For the photo, click the avatar or **Change photo**, then pick an image from your
   device. It is automatically cropped and resized before upload, so it stays light. If a
   photo is already set, you can **Remove** it.
4. Click **Save**.

> Worth knowing: your **email address** and your **role** are shown here but cannot be
> changed from this card. Email is changed in its own card (see further down); the role
> is managed by the administration team.

### Your neighborhood

Just below, the **Neighborhood** card reminds you which neighborhood you are linked to
and lets you **correct your address** at any time, with the help of autocompletion. How
the linking works in detail (address covered or "waiting for coverage") is described on
the **Create an account and sign in** page.

## The Security card

This card gathers two things: **changing your password** and the state of your
**two-factor authentication**.

### Changing your password

To set a new password, fill in:

1. your **current password**;
2. your **new password** (at least eight characters);
3. the **confirmation** of that new password;
4. the **six-digit code** from your authenticator app.

A **strength indicator** rates your new password live, from "very weak" to "strong", to
help you pick a solid one. The **Update password** button stays disabled until the
conditions are met (password too short, confirmation not matching, or incomplete code).

If an error message appears, it tells you exactly what to fix:

- **current password is incorrect**: check what you typed;
- **invalid six-digit code**: wait for a new code to appear in your app, then try again.

### Two-factor authentication

The card confirms that your **two-factor authentication is active**. It is enabled as
soon as your account is created and stays on permanently: it is what protects your access
and validates each of your sensitive actions.

In practice, for every important operation, the application asks you for the six-digit
code. The table below sums up the actions concerned.

| Action | Six-digit code required? |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Change your first name, last name or photo | No |
| Correct your address | No |
| Change your password | Yes |
| Change your email address | Yes |
| Change or remove your phone number | Yes |
| Delete your account | Yes |

## Changing your email address

Your email address is also the identifier you sign in with. To change it, open the
**Email address** card and click **Edit**. A dialog asks you for:

1. your **new email address**;
2. your current **password**;
3. the **six-digit code**.

Once the change is validated, since your sign-in identifier has changed, you are
**automatically signed out** and invited to **sign in again** with your new address. This
is normal, and it is an extra safeguard.

If the address you enter is **already used** by another account, a message warns you and
the change is refused. Pick a different address in that case.

## Changing or removing your phone number

The phone number is **optional**. From the **Phone** card, click **Edit**. In the dialog:

1. enter your number in **international format**, for example `+33612345678`;
2. enter the **six-digit code**;
3. confirm with **Save**.

If the number format is not recognised, a message asks you to fix it before you can save.
To **remove** your number, open the same dialog, **clear the field**, enter your code,
then save: your number is deleted from your account.

## Two-factor authentication: phone lost or replaced

Your authenticator app produces the six-digit code asked for at every sign-in and every
sensitive action. That **secret** never changes: it was shown to you **only once, when
your account was created**, as a QR code and as text to copy. Planning ahead is therefore
the best protection.

- **You are changing phones**: while you still have access to your old device, install an
  authenticator app on the new one and **add the same secret** to it (the one you kept
  when you signed up). Both devices will then produce the same code, and you can part
  with the old one with peace of mind.
- **A code is refused**: the code is renewed every thirty seconds. Check that your phone's
  time is set **automatically**, wait for the next code to appear, then try again.

> Useful habit: keep the secret shown at sign-up somewhere safe (screenshot, password
> manager, secure note). It is what lets you set up your two-factor authentication again
> on a new device.

If you have lost both your app **and** the original secret, you can no longer produce a
code and therefore can no longer sign in on your own: **get in touch with the platform's
administration team** to restore your access.

## Privacy (GDPR)

The last card puts you in charge of your data. It offers two clearly separate actions:
**exporting** your data or **deleting** your account.

### Exporting your data

The **Export my data** button gathers all your information and downloads it as a **file**
named `quartierconnect-mes-donnees.json`. This file corresponds to your right to data
portability (article 20 of the GDPR).

It contains in particular:

- your **profile** and the **date of your consent**;
- your **incident reports**;
- your **points balance** and the history of your **transactions** (both sent and
  received);
- your **sent messages**, your **contracts**, your **bookings**;
- your **votes** and your **participations in the neighborhood's consultations**;
- the **services** you have published.

Some sensitive information **never** appears in this export: your password (which is only
ever stored encrypted anyway) and your two-factor authentication secret.

### Deleting your account

Deletion is **permanent**. From the card, click **Delete my account**: a dialog asks you
to confirm by entering your **six-digit code**. This confirmation prevents any accidental
or malicious deletion.

As soon as you confirm, you are **immediately signed out**. Your account is then
**anonymized**: your email address is replaced by a meaningless identifier, your password
and your two-factor authentication secret are erased, and your profile no longer appears
as yours on the platform. The content you published may remain in anonymized form, for as
long as neighborhood life and moderation require.

## In short

| Card | What you manage there | Six-digit code |
|------|-----------------------|----------------|
| Profile | First name, last name, photo | No |
| Neighborhood | Your address and your link to a neighborhood | No |
| Security | Password, two-factor authentication | Yes (password) |
| Email address | Sign-in email (then sign in again) | Yes |
| Phone | Optional number (added or removed) | Yes |
| Privacy (GDPR) | Export and deletion of your data | Yes (deletion) |

You stay in control of your identity, the security of your access and what becomes of
your data, at any time and in a few clicks.
