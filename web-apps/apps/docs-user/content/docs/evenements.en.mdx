---
title: Neighborhood events
description: Discover, create and join events
---

The **Events** page brings all the local life together in one place: the gatherings,
the outings and the get-togethers organized near you. You find out what is happening in
the neighborhood, you put forward your own ideas and you let others know that you plan
to come.

## What an event looks like

Every event shown on the page carries three essential pieces of information:

- a **location**, so you know where to go;
- a **date**, so you don't miss anything;
- a **category**, which helps you sort out what interests you.

The available categories are the following:

| Category | Example gatherings |
|----------|--------------------|
| Culture     | exhibitions, concerts, workshops |
| Sport       | sports activities and outings |
| Community   | get-togethers between neighbors, friendly moments |
| Education   | educational workshops, talks, introductory sessions |
| Other       | anything that does not fit the previous categories |

## Browsing events

You can look through events in two ways, whichever suits you best:

- the **list view**, which lays out the events one after another;
- the **calendar view**, which places them on their date so you can see the busy days at
  a glance.

You can switch from one to the other at any time, without losing your place.

A playful way to discover events is to browse them by **swiping**: you go through the
suggestions one by one, like flipping through cards, until you find the ones you like.

> A preview of **upcoming events** also appears directly on your dashboard, as soon as
> you land on the platform. It is a good starting point for spotting the next
> get-togethers without even opening the Events page.

## Marking your interest

When an event appeals to you, you can mark your **interest** with a single gesture. It
is your way of saying "I plan to go" and of encouraging the organizer.

Your interest is not just for you: it feeds into **neighborhood life** and into your
personalized **recommendations**. In other words, the more you signal what you like, the
more the platform suggests events that match what you are after.

## Proposing a new event

You are not just a spectator: you can also **propose a new event** to the neighborhood.
An idea for an outing, a workshop to share, a get-together between neighbors? Create the
event to make it visible to all residents.

When creating it, fill in the information that will help your neighbors find their way:

1. A clear **title**, to identify the event at a glance.
2. The **date** and time it will take place.
3. The **location** where it will be held.
4. A **description** (optional) to give the useful details.

> In the application, the **category** is not asked for at creation time: your event is
> published in the general **Other** category. The categories presented above are there
> above all to classify and find gatherings.

Once published, your event joins the Events page and the dashboard of residents, who can
in turn mark their interest.

## In short

- Look through events in **list view** or **calendar view**, or browse them by
  **swiping**.
- Every event states a **location**, a **date** and a **category**.
- Mark your **interest** to signal that you are coming and to refine your
  recommendations.
- **Propose** your own events to liven up the neighborhood.

Events exist so people can meet: browse the page regularly and don't hesitate to let
your neighbors know about your ideas for get-togethers.
